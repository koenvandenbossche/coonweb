The Aufguss Ritual

More than just a sauna session — it's an art form, a ceremony, an experience.

1

Prepare

Lay your Aufguss towel on the bench. Let your body acclimate to the warmth of the sauna.

2

Infuse

Pour water infused with essential oils over the hot stones. Watch the steam rise and fill the room.

3

Wave

The Aufguss master waves the towel, directing the aromatic steam toward the guests in rhythmic motions.

4

Relax

Wrap yourself in your bathrobe and let the deep warmth and aromas restore your body and mind.
